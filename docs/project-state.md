# wiiuport — project state

Factual capability inventory. Epic intent is in `docs/project-goals.md`.
Every item is `verified`, `partial`, `blocked`, or `missing`.

**Current focus.** The title's rate in heavy scenes (ST-60): on the island the title keeps
29.4 to 29.6 Hz with interpolation on against 30 Hz off, and each tick it misses is shown as
one frame held 33 ms; the rendering thread's per-draw bookkeeping is what remains to cut.
The camera and every object drawn through uniform blocks interpolate continuously on the real
title: before every guest swap the runtime replays the finished frame with the view and each
object blended half way from the previous frame, presents it, puts the guest's frame back, and
lets the guest's own swap show it. Measured swimming on the GPU: 715 of 724 ticks interpolated,
the title at 28.24 Hz with interpolation on against 29.99 Hz off in alternating windows of the
same walk, frame time p50 16.7 ms and p99 34.6 ms.

## Comparison baseline

**Baseline: upstream Cemu (`cemu-project/Cemu`), used as a standalone application.**
A user today runs the Cemu AppImage, points it at a WUX, and plays at the title's own
simulation rate with community graphic packs for resolution and shading. Upstream offers
no mechanism to re-issue a frame with substituted transform state, so a 30 Hz title
presents at 30 Hz. Every item below states its difference from that baseline.

| ST-LIB | Runtime exposed to a consuming title through a narrow C++ interface | verified | The shell is split into the `wiiuport::host` library, which has everything but an entry point, and `shell/RunProduct.h`: a title project's `main` is one call, `runProduct(Product{name, title}, argc, argv)`. `Product` is the whole of a title's say over the host: its name, on the window and the first-run setup screen, and the one title it runs.
